## Prerequisites by model type

| Model | What can be done now | Required before the intended inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual or occupational | Retrieve studies, characterize agents/tasks and identify screening/cohort access routes | Permitted individual data; cancer diagnosis/death definition; employment/task and dose histories; entry/exit/person-time; residence/migration; confounders; linkage quality and consent/access safeguards. Distinguish workers from neighbors; address healthy-worker selection |
| County ecological rate association | Describe existing rates/uncertainty and source-record coverage; formulate prespecified hypotheses | Cancer-specific county outcomes if hypothesized; historically aligned pathway-based exposure; measured confounders with compatible years; justified geographic aggregation and sensitivity analysis. A precision-aware rate model is possible only after explicitly handling rate uncertainty; it would remain ecological |
| Annual/longitudinal model | Inspect raw TRI reporting history and identify coverage/definition changes | Repeated exact cancer counts and matched annual population strata; harmonized exposures and identity history; cancer/agent-specific latency rationale; nonoverlapping validation periods; handling screening/diagnostic/reporting changes. AAPC/trend labels are not annual observations |
| Spatial Poisson/NB/BYM2 count model | Design specification and data validation | Exact integer outcomes and person-time or expected counts; chosen reference rates; validated county geometry/adjacency and FIPS order; connected-component/scaling checks; priors, confounders and diagnostics. AirToxScreen population cannot substitute for cancer-year demographic denominators |

For count models, **exact counts and aligned denominators/expected counts are missing from the prepared county comparison schema**. Do not multiply age-adjusted rates by population, round modeled risks into cases, or recover “exact” five-year counts from rounded average annual counts. The period mismatch also means mortality/incidence is not a survival estimator. [NCI age adjustment](https://seer.cancer.gov/seerstat/tutorials/aarates/definition.html), [CDC expected counts/SIR](https://www.cdc.gov/cancer-environment/php/guidelines/statistical-considerations.html)

## Required harmonization and inference boundaries

- **TRI absence is not zero.** Preserve measured/reported zero, Form A unquantified certification, not-listed chemical, below-threshold/ineligible operation, missing record and unknown status separately. Reporting thresholds, sector coverage, exemptions and activity matter. [EPA factors guide](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-09/factors-to-consider_fall-2025.pdf)
- **Chemical lists change.** Use a chemical ID/CAS/category and first/last reporting-year ledger, including qualifier changes. Identical 122-column export schemas do not prove comparable historical coverage. Verify classification/version before interpreting the modern OSHA carcinogen flag as a historical designation. EPA's current changes ledger is dated **October 13, 2026**; the landing page labels the download October 14. [Original EPA ledger](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-10/tri-chemical-list-changes-10-13-2026.pdf)
- **Units and pathways remain distinct.** Sixteen 2024 TRI records use grams, other records pounds. Do not combine air/water/land/off-site transfers as one exposure, or count treatment/recycling as equivalent to releases. Title V says **“tons”; short ton versus metric tonne is unconfirmed in its PDF**, as preserved in detailed provenance/CSV. Apply no conversion until verified. HAP/VOC/particulate categories overlap; HAP mass is not carcinogen mass or risk. [DNR original inventory](https://www.iowadnr.gov/media/2277/download?inline=)
- **Identity joins need verification.** DNR inventory IDs, Facility Explorer site IDs, TRI IDs and FRS IDs are different systems. Use dated aliases, location and verified crosswalks. County names in TRI are not yet a validated FIPS join. Do not infer a county from DNR ID digits or join companies by similar names alone.
- **Latency needs history.** No universal lag is valid across cancers/agents. Keep diagnosis/death years separate from exposure, operation and sampling years. A 2024 release cannot be treated as a measured cause of a 2019 diagnosis. Lagging incomplete history does not reconstruct missing exposures. [CDC design guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/cancer-environment/php/guidelines/phase-3.html)
- **AirToxScreen is a screening model.** It uses modeled outdoor concentrations and lifetime assumptions for one analysis year. Its 2020 population and per-million lifetime risks are not annual person-time or observed cancer rates. County totals are rounded; component decimals are not equivalent scientific precision. Secondary formation is not direct attribution to one industry. [EPA limitations](https://www.epa.gov/AirToxScreen/airtoxscreen-limitations)
- **A model cannot repair unsupported exposure assignment.** County spatial effects, adjustment, low p-values, FDR control or prediction accuracy do not show that people with cancer were exposed. Control analytical multiplicity across cancer sites, chemicals, buffers, lags and subgroups; use held-out spatial/time validation matching the objective. See `modeling-methods.md` for assumptions and sources.
