# Where this research can go

Reviewed November 10, 2026. These are proposed routes, not accepted collaborations or submissions. No researcher has been contacted, no consultation purchased, and no manuscript submitted.

The strongest present contribution is a reproducible investigation of **what must be measured before interpreting a cancer-state signature or recovery curve**. Completed audits show that equal-depth thinning does not remove the signature's kit-stratum reversal and that an average signature score does not establish coordinated expression. A synthetic recovery study preserves a failed sparse design (V1) and derives a conditional measurement-precision rule (V2). These results can help prevent misleading conclusions and wasteful experiments. They do not establish a new resistance mechanism, treatment, or cure; scientific novelty remains unestablished. [Depth review](depth-simulation/independent-review.md), [joint-expression audit](coexpression/README.md), [V1](mechanistic-simulation/README.md), [V2 protocol](mechanistic-simulation/v2/protocol.json), [V2 results](mechanistic-simulation/v2/results.json).

The completed [GSE292469 analysis](../results/gse292469-alveolar-2026-11-10/RESULTS.md) compares 26 untreated with 9 residual-disease LUAD patients: residual-minus-untreated mean score −0.2631, rank AUC 0.2179. The small source-implied v4 subset reverses direction. Strong stage/site imbalance, inconsistent kit descriptions, and raw versus ambient-corrected expression prevent a biological interpretation. These observations define the following narrow opportunities.

## 1. Original MSK investigators: resolve the measurement question

**Best immediate scientific destination:** the GSE292469 study team, with Helena Yu listed as the GEO contact and Sohrab Shah among its contributors. The [Shah laboratory](https://www.mskcc.org/research-areas/labs/sohrab-shah) studies cancer evolution and cellular dynamics using single-cell genomics; [Yu's clinical research](https://www.mskcc.org/cancer-care/doctors/helena-yu) concerns lung cancer and targeted-treatment resistance. Their connection to this dataset, rather than a presumed willingness to collaborate, makes this the highest-priority route. [Primary GEO record](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE292469).

**Contribution to offer:** a compact reproduction packet containing exact gene identity, specimen-level exclusions, both directions of the kit-stratified result, independently checked arithmetic, equal-depth controls and the joint-expression diagnostic. Ask whether these observations persist in the authors' intended expression representation. Keep the separate synthetic recovery model out of any claim about these patients: cross-sectional expression does not identify its growth or switching parameters.

**Specific missing inputs:** an authoritative library-to-chemistry map; the processed `RNAsoup` matrix and its processing/version information, or the permitted inputs needed to reconstruct it; available contamination estimates; and clarification of specimen/treatment labels. The public raw UMI matrix alone is not the corrected object used by the plotting code. This request does not require patient identities. See the project's [source review](../updates/2026-11-10/independent-cohort-review.md) and [pinned author repository](https://github.com/shahcompbio/egfr_nucseq/tree/c4211458564c92b32c1276badaed2e6731ad1a68).

**Access and cost:** scientific engagement and additional data availability are unconfirmed; no service price or obligation has been identified. A public deposit does not guarantee access to every unpublished intermediate object. The [one-page brief](proposal.md) is prepared for human review, not sent.

## 2. University of Iowa: independent methods review and study design

**Practical local route:** the [Iowa Institute of Human Genetics Bioinformatics and Biostatistics Division](https://humangenetics.medicine.uiowa.edu/bioinformatics-and-biostatistics-division), followed by a Holden-affiliated collaboration if a substantive cancer question survives the measurement audit. IIHG lists 10x single-cell analysis, custom computational work and experimental-design consultation. Its [billing page](https://humangenetics.medicine.uiowa.edu/bioinformatics-and-biostatistics-division/bioinformatics-services-and-pricing) explicitly provides for off-campus accounts. However, its [consultation form](https://humangenetics.medicine.uiowa.edu/form/ask-a-bioinformatician) requests a PI and RAMS account; onboarding an unaffiliated project must be established before assuming access.

**Contribution to offer:** the auditable code, controls and held-out synthetic benchmarks. Request review of the estimand, patient-level uncertainty, confounding/identifiability, assay comparisons and the V2 precision rule. Under its idealized measurement model, three equally spaced absolute-state measurements with 10% CV and six independent replicate wells per time require a log-curvature contrast of about 0.2795 for 80% power. A useful first collaboration would assess whether a real assay can support the required state definition, precision, variance model and independent replicates. The [Holden Biostatistics Core](https://holden.uihealthcare.org/research/shared-research-resources/biostatistics-core), directed by [Brian J. Smith](https://www.public-health.uiowa.edu/people/brian-smith/), explicitly supports Holden investigators; it is not verified as a free service for independent outsiders. A genuine faculty collaboration would provide an appropriate route to that core.

**Missing validation:** source-corrected expression and genuinely independent patient data for the signature track; a validated functional state label, pilot assay-error estimates and independent scientific review for the recovery-design track. The 17-gene expression score is not such a validated label. Time-varying growth and marker retention can exactly mimic the modeled state trajectory; greater power does not solve that specificity problem. If an experimental partner is eventually needed, [Adam Dupuy's program](https://biomed.medicine.uiowa.edu/people/current-faculty/adam-dupuy) has relevant single-cell/targeted-resistance work, but its published BRAF/MAPK melanoma work is an adjacent fit, not evidence of an available EGFR-lung collaboration.

**Cost:** IIHG is a paid/collaborative service. Its published bulk-RNA example is not a quote for this single-cell project. No applicable single-cell price, free consultation, funding, capacity, or completion date is confirmed. No form has been submitted.

## 3. A completed reproducibility/methods paper: PLOS ONE

**Publication fit:** [PLOS ONE's criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) explicitly allow technically sound negative/null results and methods/software research. A controlled benchmark showing when a signature is unstable could remain useful even if every proposed new mechanism fails. The journal does not accept a bare hypothesis/proposal as an ordinary research article.

**Needed before submission:** benchmarks and controls now exist, but they need accountable human scientific review, comparison with established methods and a justified explicitly methodological scope or independent empirical evaluation. Preserve V1's failure and V2's specificity counterexamples. Add a reproducible environment, source-linked data access and the required declarations. An internal AI-agent arithmetic check is not scientific peer review. [Authorship requirements](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/authorship) include responsibility for the work and a corresponding-author ORCID. [AI policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice) requires disclosure of research/writing assistance; AI cannot be an author.

**Sharing and cost:** resolve licensing of original project code before treating GitHub visibility as unrestricted reuse. PLOS requires availability of the code underlying findings and an appropriate [data availability statement](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability); upstream data terms remain applicable. See [code-sharing policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/materials-software-and-code-sharing). An APC is payable upon acceptance; the exact applicable amount was not conclusively verified from the publisher's text-only fee table. Check the [current fee schedule](https://plos.org/fees/) before submission. Fee assistance is case-by-case and must be requested at submission, not after acceptance. No submission or payment is authorized by this destination assessment.

**Conditional preprint route:** bioRxiv permits biological reanalyses with results, subject to [screening](https://connect.biorxiv.org/news/2022/06/13/screening_procedures). Its **October 20, 2026 policy** requires verifiable human authors affiliated with an organization accountable for misconduct, plus human intellectual input and review of AI-assisted work. Therefore this independent project cannot assume present eligibility. A genuine qualifying collaboration and author review must precede that route; no nominal affiliation should be invented. [Current bioRxiv AI/accountability policy](https://connect.biorxiv.org/news/2026/10/20/approach_to_ai). Current preprint fees were not verified.

## Concrete next validation resource

The same investigators deposited **[GSE342824](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE342824)**, public November 1, 2026: paired untreated/progression Xenium sections from five patients, with each pair on one slide. This offers an orthogonal spatial measurement, **not residual-disease validation and not an independent study**.

Direct inspection of its [deposited panel](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE342nnn/GSE342824/suppl/GSE342824_gene_panel.json.gz) found **8 of the 17 signature genes**: AGER, HOPX, NAPSA, NKX2-1, SCGB3A1, SFTPB, SFTPC and SFTPD. Nine are absent. Do not call an eight-gene spatial measurement the original signature or replace unmeasured genes with zeros. No spatial expression analysis has been run.

A useful next experiment is to freeze a separate spatial question: do these measured alveolar transcripts localize to confidently annotated malignant cells, and are conclusions stable to segmentation boundaries and exclusion of neighboring normal alveolar cells? Audit the panel, annotations, patient overlap, tissue sites and permitted reuse first. Keep the five patient pairs as the independent units; progression must stay separate from residual disease. A later prospective validation would need comparable pretreatment/residual specimens and technical replicates across processing methods, designed with an actual research partner. The current dataset cannot supply those missing MRD pairs.

For the synthetic recovery result, the next practical step is an **assay-feasibility study**, not a treatment experiment: estimate time-specific error, zero-count behavior, correlation between measurements and label stability using an existing permitted model/data source with a defensible functional state definition. Recalculate the power rule using those measured errors before choosing replication. Three-point curvature tests constant net state growth; additional orthogonal measurements are needed to separate switching, growth and marker changes. The current spatial cohort and signature scores do not supply these longitudinal functional measurements.

The accompanying [machine-readable record](research-destinations.json) preserves source dates, retrieval limitations and panel hashes. The immediately usable deliverable is the reproducibility packet and validation design; collaborator interest, publication acceptance, funding and a new biological discovery remain unestablished.
