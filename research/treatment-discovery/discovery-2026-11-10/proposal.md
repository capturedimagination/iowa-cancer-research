# Research brief: what measurements can support a cancer-state claim?

Prepared November 10, 2026. Draft for human scientific review; not submitted or sent.

**Question.** What can a cancer-state expression score or a sparse recovery curve actually identify? The contribution is a reproducible measurement audit and experiment-design benchmark. Scientific novelty and clinical benefit are unestablished.

**What is available.** The exact 17-gene signature has been checked against the original supplement and author code. In public GSE292469 raw nuclear UMI data, 26 untreated and 9 residual-disease patients meet the frozen primary criteria. The residual-minus-untreated mean score is −0.2631 and the rank AUC is 0.2179. The small source-implied v4 subset points the other way. Independent arithmetic review reproduced the calculations. The cohort is unmatched, with major stage/site/kit imbalance; Series and GSM descriptions disagree about chemistry. Author figures use an ambient-corrected assay, while this calculation uses the public raw matrix. [Results, limitations and reproduction](../results/gse292469-alveolar-2026-11-10/RESULTS.md).

**Completed measurement audit.** Equal-depth thinning retains the opposite kit-stratum directions. Shuffling each gene's values within patient/depth bins leaves average signature scores unchanged while changing cell-score variability and detection breadth. This separates marginal expression from coordinated expression; neither diagnostic identifies its biological cause. First request: confirm library chemistry and obtain the intended ambient-corrected representation or permitted reconstruction inputs. [Depth review](depth-simulation/independent-review.md), [joint-expression analysis](coexpression/README.md).

**Completed synthetic design study.** None of the 81 primary hypothetical cases reaches 80% detection under V1's sparse designs; its negative result is retained. V2 examines absolute abundance of an independently defined functional state at three equally spaced times. Its log-curvature contrast is `C = 2 mean(log X(T)) − mean(log X(0)) − mean(log X(2T))`. Under independent, known constant-CV lognormal measurement error, its noise SD is `sqrt(6 log(1+CV²)/r)`. At 10% CV and six independent replicate wells per time, a contrast of about **0.2795** gives 80% power for its two-sided 5% test. This is conditional design guidance, not a clinical schedule or fitted patient model. [V1](mechanistic-simulation/README.md), [V2 specification](mechanistic-simulation/v2/protocol.json), [V2 results](mechanistic-simulation/v2/results.json).

**What would make this usable.** A partner could assess an existing assay's functional state definition, time-specific error, zero counts, independent replication and label stability, then revise the design calculation using measured uncertainty. Curvature only challenges constant net state growth. Time-varying growth or marker retention can exactly mimic the modeled trajectory, so additional orthogonal measurements are essential. The 17-gene score is not assumed to identify functionally tolerant cells.

**Orthogonal follow-up.** GSE342824 contains five paired untreated/progression Xenium cases. Its panel covers only eight of the 17 genes. A separate, explicitly partial-marker analysis could test malignant-cell localization and segmentation sensitivity; it cannot validate the full signature, the MRD endpoint, or independent-patient generalization. [Resource audit and sources](research-destinations.md#concrete-next-validation-resource).

**Useful outcome.** A tested workflow identifying insufficient evidence, plus a quantitative precision requirement to examine before collecting data. Keep the empirical expression audit and hypothetical dynamics model separate; neither establishes a resistance mechanism or therapeutic target.

**Collaboration sought, if authorized later.** Dataset-owner clarification first; independent single-cell/statistical review second; a prospectively designed paired validation only if justified. The project can supply code, hashes, exclusions, aggregate tables and simulation outputs. Human investigators would need to review and take responsibility for any manuscript. No funding, institutional affiliation, data-access entitlement or clinical efficacy is claimed. [Ranked destinations and current requirements](research-destinations.md).
