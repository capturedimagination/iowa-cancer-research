## Concrete next application

**Question:** among eligible recorded newborn sister pairs, does a model using cell age and measured birth/calendar context predict held-out subtree survival as well as a model additionally using maternal history?

Use an observed division as the sampling unit, label the target as division-pair-selected, and cluster uncertainty by original recorded lineage and experiment. Define equal elapsed daughter follow-up using birth and predetermined administrative support only. Never require later division, survival, or complete descendants for eligibility. A recorded-at-birth target does not require every original founder, but it does require future-independent inclusion of the recorded births. That requirement has not yet been established for this export.

Start with the three control sheets as an observation-model pilot after the dictionary is fixed. Preserve every known death and unknown/lost branch. Freeze lags, timing tolerances, graph rules, split units, predictors, and evaluation criteria before computing the proposed outcomes. Use held-out sheets only after identifying their biological/technical relationship; three sheets provide limited experiment-level validation. Keep the ordinary within-bin sample covariance as the simpler benchmark. An external fitted mean does not improve that target automatically, and the binomial correction does not apply wholesale to arbitrary regression.

The useful result could be adequate independent-model prediction, evidence that measured context matters, a reproducible residual prediction gap, or demonstrated lack of identifiability/precision. A residual family association alone cannot separate transmitted state from persistent shared family environment. A horizon contrast cannot by itself establish inheritance, and a coarse-bin covariance is not exact-birth conditional dependence. Do not claim a cancer mechanism or treatment benefit from any of these outcomes.

## Conditional manuscript routes

| Route | Present fit and requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **PLOS ONE: bounded methods/application study** | Most plausible of the journal routes assessed. It considers methods and null results, but requires original research, rigorous reporting, supported conclusions, and a clear rationale for derivative work. A generic warning or proposal alone is insufficient. A completed reproducible study may stand on computational evidence if its claims remain computational; an admissible empirical application would make the usefulness clearer. [Official criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) |
| **eLife: public expert assessment** | A possible later route for a focused contribution to lineage measurement and inference. Editors still select which manuscripts receive review, considering interest, reviewer expertise and value of public review. Only selected/reviewed work proceeds as a Reviewed Preprint with assessment and reviews. Do not treat that model as automatic acceptance. [Current process](https://elifesciences.org/about/peer-review), [editorial selection explanation](https://elifesciences.org/articles/100571.pdf) |
| **PLOS Computational Biology: higher methodological/biological threshold** | Not the immediate target. Its criteria require originality, innovation and significant biological or methodological insight; methods articles have a broad-utility expectation. Experimental validation is encouraged rather than universally mandatory. Repackaging known time changes, covariance identities and concentration bounds would not establish that contribution. Reconsider only if the study demonstrates substantial added capability or well-supported biological insight. [Official scope and criteria](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/s/journal-information) |

These are fit judgments, not invitations, acceptance predictions, or completed submissions. The original eLife venue and Iyer's PLOS venue do not establish a claim to publication there.

## Go/no-go gates

**Go now:** preserve the completed model/source audit, recovery and reproduction evidence; produce the source dictionary and a concrete validation packet; and specify the empirical estimand without calculating it yet.

**Go to empirical prediction only when:** cohort inclusion can be defended from birth-time/past information, loss and administrative observation are mapped or conservatively bounded, source anomalies remain explicit, and replicate/family grouping supports the frozen split. If these gates fail, retain a descriptive record/bounds exercise or design a new prospective experiment. Do not solve the problem by filtering to complete successful trees.

**Go to a methods manuscript only when:** every planned computational comparison is accounted for, the packaging failure and unchanged-draw recovery are documented, a clean reproduction works, and a human-led scientific review accepts the limited claim. This is a manuscript-readiness recommendation, not an extra approval requirement for the already authorized project work. Explain added practical value beyond the known literature, retain the sample-covariance benchmark, distinguish simulation uncertainty from biological uncertainty, and provide truthful data/code availability and authorship statements. A public repository is not itself an open-source license; repository-level licensing must be resolved for any claim of freely reusable software.

**No-go claims:** first-ever covariance correction, universal inheritance detection, refutation of Kuchen/Iyer from hypothetical controls, calibrated clinical time/dose, or cancer treatment discovery. The immediate contribution is a testable measurement/analysis workflow with explicit failure modes. The empirical question is still open.
