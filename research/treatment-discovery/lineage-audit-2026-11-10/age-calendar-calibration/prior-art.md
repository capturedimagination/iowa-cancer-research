# Bounded prior-art and application audit

Checked 2026-11-10. Planning only: no new model outcomes, empirical fitting, external contact or public edits. Fourteen targeted searches plus primary-source inspection covered sister-subtree summaries, conditional residual covariance, nuisance fitting, calibration corrections, conditional randomization and finite-sample concentration. This is not an exhaustive priority, patent or unpublished-work search. The companion JSON records URLs, access scope and limitations.

**Assessment:** sister-subtree statistics, residual-product conditional-covariance measures, nuisance-error products, unbiased squared-proportion corrections and bounded-sum concentration already have close or direct prior art. Combining age dependence with a deterministic common clock is an ordinary time change. None should be presented as a first-ever method or a cancer mechanism. A useful contribution could be a reproducible assay-design warning about shared fitted context, birth-time binning and calibration budgets, with prospective validation.

## Statistics that already exist

**Residual-product testing and fitting error.** Shah & Peters introduce the generalized covariance measure from products of conditional-mean residuals. Their Eq.5 explicitly contains a product of the two regression errors; Theorem6 imposes error-rate and moment requirements for asymptotic calibration. Auxiliary independent training is discussed separately. Our shared-fit squared-error identity is a direct special case, not a newly discovered correction to their method. Their general theory must not be relabeled as automatic finite-sample validity for our binary lineage experiment. [Annals of Statistics 48(3),1514–1538,2020, DOI10.1214/19-AOS1857](https://doi.org/10.1214/19-AOS1857); [inspected author manuscript, §3](https://arxiv.org/html/1804.07203).

**Cross-fitting is not a guarantee of zero finite-sample bias.** Chernozhukov and colleagues combine orthogonal scores, cross-fitting and nuisance-estimation conditions. Training/test separation addresses dependence and overfitting; it does not make an inaccurate common conditional-mean estimate exact. The project's proposed warning is consistent with this literature. [The Econometrics Journal 21,C1–C68,2018, DOI10.1111/ectj.12097](https://doi.org/10.1111/ectj.12097).

**A residual mean is not every conditional alternative.** The weighted GCM literature already addresses alternatives that an unweighted average residual covariance may miss. No claim that one averaged sister statistic universally detects inheritance is defensible. This pass inspected the author abstract, not every theorem or code path. [Scheidegger, Hörrmann & Bühlmann, author version2,2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04361v2).

**Known and estimated conditional laws differ.** The model-X conditional randomization test uses a known conditional law; the conditional permutation paper studies robustness to approximation error. An exact-birth Bernoulli resampling check belongs to this established family. Replacing its probability with a fitted curve can invalidate exact calibration. [Candès et al., JRSSB80,551–577,2018, DOI10.1111/rssb.12265](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12265); [Berrett et al., author manuscript, §§2 and5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05405), [published JRSSB82,175–197,2020](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssb/article/82/1/175/7056014).

**The simple binomial correction is elementary.** For independent `X~Binomial(m,p)`, `E[X(X-1)/(m(m-1))]=p²`. Consequently `phat²-phat(1-phat)/(m-1)` is unbiased for p² when m>=2. This is the ordinary distinct-observation/U-statistic construction. The classic general reference is [Hoeffding, Annals of Mathematical Statistics19,293–325,1948, DOI10.1214/aoms/1177730196](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177730196); publisher/archive bibliographic details were verified, but its full text was not retrieved here. The Bernoulli identity above is independently derived algebra, not a claim that this exact application appears in that paper.

**Finite bounds are classical.** The proposed known-variance/range validation uses established bounded independent-sum inequalities. Neither the concentration inequality nor its use in Monte Carlo verification is a novelty claim. [Bennett, JASA57,33–45,1962](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1962.10482149), [Hoeffding, JASA58,13–30,1963](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830). Current publisher abstracts and bibliographic records were inspected; the precise proposed bounds also require independent algebra/code review before freeze.

## Current related work

[He et al., *On the Hardness of Conditional Independence Testing In Practice*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14000) identifies conditional-mean-embedding errors as a practical source of type-I error problems. Its author page reports NeurIPS2025 publication. This pass inspected the abstract/publication metadata, not the entire experimental pipeline; it is further evidence that nuisance-related false positives are a current established concern.

[Thams & Lundborg, *Embedding-Bias in Conditional Independence Testing*, submitted8October2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11584) studies residual tests after replacing context by a coarser representation. Its abstract identifies bias from conditional-mean components lost by that representation. This is a **preprint**, and only its abstract was inspected. Birth-time binning is a related coarsening issue, not evidence that our proposed application is the paper's exact setting or a new general result.

## Closest cancer-lineage evidence

[Korsnes & Korsnes, *Initial refinement of data from video-based single-cell tracking*,2023, DOI10.1002/cai2.88](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10686135/) is close prior art for sister-subtree summaries in A549 tracking. The current primary abstract was accessible. The earlier project audit inspected its paired-descendant and track-length figures and Discussion; this pass's later full-text requests challenged/failed. Exact extant-versus-cumulative descendant semantics and full eligibility rules remain unresolved, so it is not automatically our binary fixed-lag survival variable.

[Kuchen et al., *Hidden long-range memories of growth and cycle speed correlate cell cycles in lineage trees*, eLife9:e51002,2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7018508/) models inherited cycle/growth effects and discusses circadian alternatives. Cycle-length correlations and our prospective subtree-survival indicators differ, but inherited and shared-context explanations are already a developed experimental/modeling topic.

[Iyer et al., *Inheritable cell-states shape drug-persister correlations and population dynamics in cancer cells*,2025, DOI10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013446](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013446) combines time-lapse data, competing cell fates and state inheritance. Its age-structured baseline was already checked in the project's earlier Methods/supplement audit. An age/calendar counterexample for our observation rule does not show that this study omitted age dependence or refute its empirical inference. This pass refreshed main-text publication/author information; no new supplement/code reproduction is claimed.

## What would make this useful experimentally

The narrow deliverable is a **calibration and context checklist backed by explicit model calculations**, not a new covariance estimator. It should distinguish: true within-context sister dependence; residual variation within coarse birth-time bins; and shared probability-estimation noise. Include ordinary sample covariance as a no-calibration benchmark whenever the target is simply bin-level covariance. Calibrating p externally is not necessary for that basic target.

A useful prospective test would collect complete sister subtrees with birth time, treatment/medium clock, stage reporter and field/well metadata; retain missing outcomes separately from death; and use independent movies/wells for nuisance training. Randomizing treatment timing across wells and measuring the resulting context can constrain particular shared-clock explanations. The full calibration sample, target founder and experimental-well structure must enter uncertainty. Shared family environment or unmeasured context remains an alternative to inheritance.

The [current official NCBS research page for Shaon Chakrabarti](https://www.ncbs.res.in/node/80022) describes cancer-cell time-lapse imaging, age-structured population models, circadian context and cell-fate decisions. That makes this group a concrete candidate for scientific validation of a reviewed packet and experimental design, not an established collaborator. The source-encoding questions from the HCT116 audit still need resolution before reanalysis. No data access, contact, manuscript submission or acceptance has been obtained.

## Novelty and access boundary

No inspected source established priority for this exact combined synthetic calibration-budget exercise. That absence is weak evidence: the key mathematical components and closely related assays are established, and the bounded audit did not inspect every supplement, implementation, thesis or unpublished experiment. The useful claim should be a transparent application/measurement warning, subject to independent validation. The remaining scientific work is demonstrating that the issue matters in a correctly encoded, prospectively observed experiment.

Some arXiv HTML renderings display a2026 rendering date despite older version/publication histories; this audit uses the article's version history and journal metadata rather than treating that rendering date as a new paper. Publisher redirects/PMC challenges were recorded, not bypassed. Only primary papers, author manuscripts and official institutional pages support substantive claims here.
