## Useful application, including a negative result

Freeze the retained pair roster, elapsed lags, event-boundary alternatives and shared-descendant constraints before deriving any outcome mask. Ask: **which covariance values are compatible with those recorded pairs and the declared observation assumptions?** Report ordered and symmetrized targets separately. A range spanning zero may reflect missing information or a loose relaxation; it does not prove an attainable zero completion, biological independence or fundamental impossibility. Even a positive lower endpoint is a finite-roster property, not evidence distinguishing inherited state from shared environment or export selection.

If source clarification supports prospective recorded births, a later collaboration could collect or validate birth-defined sister follow-up and compare age/calendar-context predictions with maternal-history predictions. If clarification remains unavailable, a reproducible retained-record audit and benchmark still have practical value. Neither path requires selecting only complete or successful trees.
