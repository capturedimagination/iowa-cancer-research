## Scope and estimands

The 72 frozen cases retain all founders, including extinct and never-dividing families. A chronological tag chooses daughters prospectively and fairly. The selected survivor function is `Q_T(t)=E[F_T U(t)]/P(F_T)`, for `t<=T`; it is neither the survival of an endpoint-selected descendant nor an equal-terminal-cell average. Horizon contrasts compare the same absolute `t` and founder law. The sister statistic uses at most one prospectively eligible first-division pair per founder, with equal follow-up lag. These definitions agree between the protocol and the core.

The core dispatches the declared 63 Erlang cases and nine non-Erlang controls. The two k=1 initializations are intentionally duplicated. Uniform initial stages are a specified mixture of residual stage counts, not an equilibrium age distribution. Every new daughter starts at stage 1. The independent daughter-type draws encoded by `K=rho*I+(1-rho)/types` are essential: a jointly redrawn sister type would have different equations.

## Hand-algebra checks

1. **Family survival** (`age_lineage_models.py:39`). With `g_z=sum_v K_zv h_v1`, the last-stage branching term is `alpha_z*(2*g_z-g_z^2)`. The two daughters' futures are independent conditional on the maternal type. Internal stage transitions and the common death rate have the correct signs. Initial founder mixtures are applied to probabilities before forming the selected ratio.

2. **Positive marked joint probability** (`:56`). Put `r=T-t` and `J_zj(x;r)=P(tag alive at x-r, family alive at x)`. At `x=r`, `J=h(r)`. At a division, partition on the discarded sister's survival. If she is extinct, the tagged daughter's joint event is required; if she survives, only the tag's survival is required. This yields `(1-g_z)*Jbar_z + exp[-mu*(x-r)]*g_z`, exactly the code. There is no factor two because the tagged daughter is chosen fairly. Common constant mortality makes the unselected tag survival `exp(-mu*t)` even through division and type changes. At `t=T`, `J=exp(-mu*T)` gives the endpoint identity; at `t=0`, `Q=1`.

3. **Positive death defect and scaling** (`:108`). For `D=h-J`, the terminal source is `alpha_z*((1-g_z)*Dbar_z+(1-exp[-mu*(x-r)])*g_z)`. There is no separate founder-death source: death before any division leaves no surviving family. For zero-based stage `j`, use `p_j=k-j+1`, `x=T-t+t*v`, and `D_zj=t^p_j Z_zj(v)`. The internal transition coefficient becomes `alpha_z`; the diagonal becomes `-(mu+alpha_z)*t`; the terminal offspring coefficient is `alpha_z*t^k*(1-g_z)`; and the source is `alpha_z*(-expm1(-mu*t*v)/t)*g_z`. These match the implementation, including the exponent and the stable evaluation of the small source. Reconstruction uses the same stage powers. It avoids subtracting a nearly unit probability to obtain a tiny loss.

4. **Qualifying maternal weights and covariance** (`:84`). A founder starting with `r` exponential stages remaining divides before death and cutoff `B` with probability `(alpha/(alpha+mu))^r * P(Gamma(r,alpha+mu)<=B)`. Averaging this quantity under the initial stage/type law produces the actual eligible-pair type weights. They are generally not the initial one-half weights. Conditional on maternal type, independent daughter futures have mean `g_z(s)=(K h_newborn(s))_z`; therefore covariance is `Var_w[g_z(s)]`. For two types, `g_0-g_1=rho*(h_01-h_11)`, so the stored `rho^2*w_0*w_1*(h_01-h_11)^2` identity is correct. This does not imply monotonic covariance in rho because the subtree probabilities also depend on rho.

5. **Deterministic-age control** (`:155`). The family recursion multiplies founder survival to division by the probability that at least one daughter subtree survives. The marked recursion partitions on the discarded sister just as above. For `t<=d`, selected-family membership forces the founder/tag alive through `t`. In `d<t<=T<2d`, the recursion reduces to `(1+exp[-mu*(t-d)]-exp[-mu*(T-d)])/(2-exp[-mu*(T-d)])`. The stated post-division boundary is consistent because chronological mass is conserved at division.

6. **Shared-calendar control** (`:191`). For `L'=lambda`, the reciprocal backward survival equation gives `h(b,T)=1/(1+mu*integral_b^T exp[-(L(u)-L(b))+mu*(u-b)]du)`. Birth time is retained; the function is not evaluated as an elapsed-age law. The selected probability `exp[-L(t)]+exp[-mu*t]*integral_0^t lambda(u)*exp[mu*u-L(u)]du` agrees with the existing horizon-invariance proof. Eligible first-division density is `lambda(b)*exp[-L(b)-mu*b]`. Pooling over this density produces covariance `Var_b[h(b,b+s)]`; birth-conditioned residual cross-product has expectation zero. A pooled raw covariance is therefore not an inheritance-specific signal.

7. **Early-time coefficient and scope** (`:139`). Newborn order is `k+1`, with coefficient `mu*sum_z p_z alpha_z^k (K h_newborn(T))_z / ((k+1)! H_initial(T))`. Uniform-stage order is 2 because the last initial stage contributes the leading term; its coefficient is `mu*sum_z p_z*(alpha_z/k)*(K h_newborn(T))_z/(2 H_initial(T))`. Homogeneous or matching fair-redraw newborn laws have the stated cancellation. Arbitrary initial type laws and uniform-stage founders need not. Mixed initial age/stage does not generally imply a linear onset. Zero death correctly returns zero defect and an undefined positive-leading-term ratio.

8. **Finite-epsilon envelope**. The supplied derivation bounds the first discarded sister below and the union of all discarded sisters above. The survival inequality `-mu*h<=h'<=0`, polynomial Erlang density bounds, and the nonnegative matrix bound `K*diag(alpha^k)*g<=beta*g` justify the exponential comparisons. On the frozen grid, `mu/alpha_max<=2` and `beta^(1/k)<=3^(1/k)*alpha_max`; the frozen `exp(-3*epsilon)` to `exp(6*epsilon)` envelope is conservative. This verifies the algebraic scope of the gate, not numerical interval certification. Neither monotone errors nor a globally equivalent multistep lifetime distribution follows.

Two execution-gate findings were sent to the root before any scientific run:

The snapshot also omits completed strict results and convergence diagnostics. If the strict evaluation itself raises, tuple assignment has not retained the already-computed standard result. Preserve an earlier failure artifact instead of overwriting it.

Some bad secondary fields could reach final `allow_nan=False` serialization outside the failure handler, losing the run evidence. Reject nonfinite numeric leaves before audits/comparisons, report exact field paths, and encode invalid values as explicit diagnostic records/strings in a failure snapshot rather than silently coercing them to zero/null. Successful outputs must remain strict JSON.

Numeric leaves receive an explicit `math.isfinite` check. Invalid values are retained as `NONFINITE:` strings with field paths. ## Claims and remaining limits

The protocol and planned result limits describe hypothetical dimensionless models, no clinical fit, no target discovery, and no first-ever theorem claim. They explicitly retain Iyer's age-dependent baselines and close sister-subtree prior art. A permanent shared family environment with the same conditional tree laws is observationally equivalent to a perfectly persistent inherited type. Covariance cannot universally distinguish those explanations. Model-based calendar residuals require known calendar-specific survival; they are not automatically available in experimental data.

The near-zero powers are local asymptotic properties of the stipulated observation scheme and initial law. They neither count biochemical killing stages nor establish a global Erlang distribution. The all-case numerical and Monte Carlo comparisons are implementation diagnostics, not biological hypothesis tests. No statement here validates empirical ascertainment, imaging completeness, or treatment efficacy.
