# Where this result can be useful

November 10, 2026. **Proposed use: a reproducible benchmark for deciding what cancer-cell lineage measurements can identify before fitting an inherited-resistance explanation.** This is a methods direction, not a validated biomarker, treatment target or claim of scientific priority. No contact or submission has occurred.

The [completed synthetic study](README.md) supplies independent renewal counterexamples, a shared-calendar covariance control, an early-time selection law, explicit observational equivalence, all fixed-grid outcomes, and retained numerical/statistical limitations. The two failed approximate Monte Carlo error checks and 168 low-precision checks belong in any review package. The exact-model comparisons passed; that does not justify describing every validation step as passed.

## Measurements a defensible application would need

| Requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A prospective founder roster, independent experiment/field IDs, and a documented observation origin | Endpoint survival, future track completeness and repeated descendants change sampling or apparent replication. |
| Known newborn status or a justified distribution of founder ages/stages | Founder initialization changes early-time order and horizon dependence, even without transmitted type. |
| Division and death timing, stable parent/child IDs, and censoring/loss reasons | A missing daughter is not an extinct subtree; both sides of a qualifying sister pair must remain accounted for. |
| Prospective sister eligibility and fixed elapsed follow-up | Selecting pairs by future fate creates a different estimand. Sister covariance and family-selected survival must use their separate inclusion rules. |
| Birth calendar time, experimental condition and relevant shared context | Pooled sisters can covary because they share a birth time or environment. Estimating a context correction introduces uncertainty absent from the exact known-model control. |
| Adequate contributing outcomes, not just a large total denominator | The present simulation flags show why selected sample size alone is not an adequate precision check for a rare difference. |

Use founder/experiment units for uncertainty. Full descendant counts do not create independent biological replicates. The present uniform-stage initialization is a sensitivity construction, not a validated age model for an experimental population.

## Next bounded challenge

Before proposing a discriminator, test an independently specified combination of non-exponential age dependence and common changing calendar context. Those ingredients were tested separately in this release and each defeats one of the simpler interpretations. Preserve the current results, freeze the combined model and measurement before computation, and include no-transmission and transmitted alternatives rather than choosing favorable examples afterward.

A useful follow-up would also compare known-context residuals with estimated-context residuals, using experiment-level holdouts and a predeclared precision analysis at finite times. Do not use the tiny-time asymptotic coefficient as an empirical endpoint merely because the mathematical limit is clean: all 180 declared near-zero positive-death calculations have far below one expected selected tagged death per 10,000 founders.

Even a successful restricted classifier would not solve the exact equivalence between a permanent inherited label and a permanent unobserved family environment with identical conditional laws. Breaking that equivalence requires additional measured variables, scientifically justified restrictions or a feasible intervention with different predictions under the competing mechanisms. No particular laboratory manipulation or clinical intervention has been validated here.

## Scientific destination

The first route remains **source-domain review with Shaon Chakrabarti's group at the National Centre for Biological Sciences**, identified through the current [official profile](https://theory.ncbs.res.in/people/shaon-chakrabarti) and the [Iyer paper](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013446). The existing [source-validation packet](../missing-branch-bounds/research-destination.md) lists the exact time, identity and event-annotation questions for the HCT116 export. Those questions remain unresolved by synthetic modeling. The PC9 and HCT116 sources are distinct biological contexts.

The offer would be a transparent reproduction and observation-model benchmark with code and counterexamples, followed by source-supported reanalysis if the required metadata can be established. It should explicitly acknowledge that Iyer already models age-dependent hazards and Korsnes already discusses sister-subtree covariance. Computational checks by separate implementations are not independent human scientific review or experimental validation.

**PLOS ONE is a conditional manuscript destination** for a technically sound methods/reanalysis study with a justified contribution; its [publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication) are documented in the earlier destination packet. Before submission, an accountable human researcher must review the mathematics, observation rules, data provenance, novelty, disclosure and authorship. Source clarification, sufficient empirical or methodological contribution, licensing, fees and journal eligibility remain separate decisions. No acceptance or collaborator participation is implied.

The public repository is the current research destination. It allows others to inspect the work now while the source-review and manuscript routes remain proposals. An external message or submission would require separate user authorization.
