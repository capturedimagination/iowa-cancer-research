# Where the HCT116 sampling audit can be useful

Assessed **November 10, 2026**. Recommended application: a reproducible benchmark that tests whether an explicit lineage-observation model predicts survival-selected family measurements. This is HCT116 colorectal-cell research, not an Iowa patient study or a treatment claim.

**The immediate deliverable is a reconstruction and validation packet.** The [resource audit](next-empirical-resource.md) supports acquisition. Empirical inference requires source-consistent tree reconstruction, event timing and founder eligibility; the linked [structural audit](hct116-structure/results.json) is preliminary. An empirical falsification result is not available yet.

## 1. Scientific validation: the source researchers

**Shaon Chakrabarti's NCBS group is the closest fit.** Its official program combines time-lapse cancer-cell measurements with population models, and Chakrabarti is the corresponding author of the 2025 reanalysis of these data. This recommendation is an inference from the group's stated work, not an invitation or agreement. [NCBS research program](https://www.ncbs.res.in/faculty/shaon-research); [Iyer et al., 2025](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013446).

Prepare one small packet for possible later review:

- A workbook-to-event-graph converter, source hashes and a source-row/frame trace for every inferred relationship.
- A founder roster and discrepancy table: candidate versus published roots, missing branches, duplicate ancestry, never-divider exclusions, entry time and treatment/frame boundaries.
- A frozen prediction protocol, independent simulation controls and every empirical result, including failed reconstructions and inconclusive comparisons.

The useful scientific request would be to verify the reconstruction dictionary and identify the intended source cohort, then assess whether the proposed held-out prediction adds anything to the existing analyses. Scientific clarification can resolve source ambiguities; it is not blanket endorsement or formal peer review. No contact has been made. Contact would require separate authorization, and prospective collaborators must consent before being named as authors.

## 2. Public use: a reconstruction and observation-model benchmark

The existing [public project](https://github.com/capturedimagination/iowa-cancer-research) is the first practical destination. Publish versioned code, documentation, eligible-founder counts, ambiguity flags, fixed simulation controls, split assignments and full result tables through the project's publication checks. A reader should be able to recreate each result from the pinned originals and distinguish a source annotation from our inference.

The original article-associated workbook and dictionary have a CC BY 4.0 reuse framework, subject to separately credited exceptions; the resource audit found no contrary notice in those files. Preserve attribution and transformation history. Source-author permission is not an extra prerequisite merely to analyze this licensed deposit. A public GitHub repository does not automatically license newly written code: the project-wide code license still needs an explicit owner decision before describing this as open-source software. No original files are mirrored by this destination note. [Original study and associated material](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-07788-5).

| Empirical outcome | Useful contribution and permissible conclusion |
|---|---|
| Sampling-aware baseline predicts held-out outcomes adequately | A calibrated reference for these outcomes in this cohort; does not establish absence of inherited states. Define adequate precision and a practical tolerance before fitting. |
| Baseline fails and a prespecified inherited-state alternative improves prediction | Evidence for extra predictive structure under the tested models; not unique proof of memory, a new mechanism or a therapeutic target. |
| Comparison is imprecise | A power/precision limitation and reusable benchmark; a nonsignificant difference does not establish equivalence. |
| Source reconstruction remains unresolved | A documented parser, discrepancy inventory and missing-data requirements. Do not fit hazards from an unverified graph. |

## 3. Publication: a completed methods/reanalysis study

**PLOS ONE is a plausible journal target after a complete, technically sound study.** It considers methods and negative/null findings, but derivative work needs a clear scientific rationale. The added value must be the verified reconstruction and a prespecified prediction test, beyond the correlations and models already examined in 2018 and 2025. This is a possible fit, not an acceptance forecast. A standalone software article has additional project-maturity requirements. [Publication criteria](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/criteria-for-publication); [software and code policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/materials-software-and-code-sharing).

Submission requires accountable human authors who contribute intellectually, review the actual analysis and approve the manuscript; a corresponding-author ORCID and submission account; transparent AI-use disclosure; and accessible supporting data/code. These steps have not been completed. AI-assisted checking is not journal peer review. [Authorship](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/authorship); [AI policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/ethical-publishing-practice); [data policy](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability).

An article-processing charge is payable on acceptance at the rate applicable when submitted. **The exact PLOS ONE amount is not certified here:** the accessible fee-table extraction omitted journal labels. Confirm the applicable quote and funding before submission. Fee assistance is assessed individually and must be requested at submission, not after acceptance; none is promised or approved. [PLOS fees and assistance](https://plos.org/fees/).

**A bioRxiv preprint is a separate, conditional dissemination step.** Its October 20, 2026 policy requires verifiable human authors affiliated with an accountable organization and meaningful human review and intellectual input. That eligibility, an account and an author-approved manuscript have not been established here. A screened preprint would not constitute peer review. Current submission-fee terms were not verified; no fee or submission commitment is made. [Current bioRxiv policy](https://connect.biorxiv.org/news/2026/10/20/approach_to_ai).

Before any empirical claim, freeze eligibility, time origin, event likelihood, model flexibility, founder-level split, prediction score and a useful precision criterion. Keep all relatives and repeated paths together; represent loss and absent branches explicitly; include never-dividers where observable; treat delayed entry separately. Test held-out predictions, not the accounting identity `Q × h = prospective tag survival`. Without experiment identifiers, conclusions remain within the recorded cohort. Source-owner review, public documentation, preprint screening and journal peer review are different stages. No investigators have been contacted and no manuscript has been submitted.
